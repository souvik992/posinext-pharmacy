# Win Indian counters with offline-first billing

A new Indian retail POS built on the Tiffin POS codebase should ship one thing competitors gate or break: **the same full billing engine running offline on both a Windows counter and an Android device, without tier gating**. Put batch/expiry/MRP, a configurable scale-barcode parser, label printing, khata (customer credit) and a GSTR-1 export in the MVP. Leave e-invoice, e-way bill, multi-store transfers, ONDC and advanced promotions for later. The market anchor is low: SME apps such as myBillBook charge **₹3,490–3,990/yr**, and they put POS mode, e-invoice, loyalty and Tally export behind a higher tier ([myBillBook pricing](https://mybillbook.in/pricing)). Zoho POS keeps offline billing off its free plan and Buy X Get Y on Premium ([Zoho POS pricing](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html)). User complaints about Vyapar, myBillBook and GoFrugal centre on **data loss, offline mode that fails or gets withdrawn, and slowness at busy counters**, not on missing features. The compliance core is heavier than in QSR and must be built in from day one: Rule 46 invoice fields and numbering, a date-effective HSN rate master (GST 2.0 slabs of **5%/18%/40% from 22 Sep 2025**), MRP held per batch as a hard price ceiling, composition-mode bills of supply, the FSSAI number on bills, and 72-month retention. Tiffin's ESC/POS, GST billing, Electron and Android shell carry over directly. The domain model is what changes most: SKUs become variant/batch/MRP/UOM aware, and stock becomes an append-only ledger.

## Offline parity and khata decide the market, not the feature list

Every Indian product surveyed lists GST billing, inventory and barcodes. They differ on POS counter mode, batch/expiry, e-invoice and e-way bill, loyalty and promotions, weighing scales, ONDC and offline operation. The price bands show where the money is. SME apps cost about ₹3–7k/yr. Desktop ERPs such as BUSY cost **₹11,000–22,000 perpetual**, with POS, batch and e-invoice starting only at the Standard edition and offline branch merge only at Enterprise ([BUSY pricing](https://busy.in/pricing/)). Tally costs ₹8,100/yr or ₹22,500 lifetime for Silver, and it offers POS only through separate Shoper editions and has no Android app on its buy page ([Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/buy-tally/)). Chain POS runs about **₹3,500 per POS user per month** at Ginesys ([Ginesys pricing](https://www.ginesys.in/pricing)), and Shopify charges **₹7,000/mo per location** for POS Pro on top of its plan ([Shopify India](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing)). Higher tiers consistently hold back the same set of features: POS mode, e-invoice and e-way bill, loyalty and promotions, multiple users and locations, and Tally export.

Offline-plus-Android is the contested ground. Vyapar is reported to work offline with sync on Android and Windows ([Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/vyapar)). GoFrugal says its POS works without internet and syncs later, and that it supports scanners, printers and weighing scales on mobile ([GoFrugal retail](https://www.gofrugal.com/retail)). Ginesys Zwing describes itself as "cloud-native with offline fallback" ([Ginesys](https://www.ginesys.in/Retail-shop-billing-software)). Users test these claims. One myBillBook reviewer said the offline app was discontinued even though offline was why they bought it ([apptail](https://apptail.io/app/mybillbook-billing-software-xWB/india)), and another wrote "offline billing is never possible" ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/202732/FloBooks/reviews/)). Credit is the other lever. OkCredit gives khata with reminders away free ([OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/khatabook-alternative)). One vendor blog claims 60–70% of kirana transactions involve udhaar; that figure is promotional, not verified ([commmerce.com](https://commmerce.com/blog/top-billing-software-indian-kirana-grocery-stores-2026/)). Either way, a POS without first-class credit tender sends the owner back to a separate khata app.

GoFrugal sells loyalty, the weighing scale, Tally integration and a stock-taking app as add-ons, and it lists offers and promotions as "coming soon" ([GoFrugal pricing](https://www.gofrugal.com/pricing.html)). GoFrugal claims native ONDC and WhatsApp ordering ([GoFrugal omnichannel](https://www.gofrugal.com/omnichannel-retail-solutions.html)). Those are real differentiators, but for chains rather than for a single kirana's first install.

Several of these rules shape the code more than the table suggests. **Retail prices are tax-inclusive, so tax is always back-calculated per line.** The formulas are taxable = price × 100 / (100 + rate) and tax = price − taxable. CGST and SGST are each half the tax, with a residual-paisa adjustment so that taxable + CGST + SGST equals the line total exactly. Keep all amounts in paise. A discount printed on the invoice reduces the taxable value (Rule 46(k)), so the discount is applied before tax. No primary source fixes invoice rounding: CBIC has no specific rule, and s.170 rounds only amounts payable to the government. A rupee "Round off" line is therefore established practice rather than law. Validate invoice numbers with `^[A-Za-z0-9/-]{1,16}$`. Generate them as prefix + FY + counter, for example `S1/2627/000123` (14 characters), with the counter resetting on 1 April. Give each offline terminal its own series so numbers never collide. GST has no provision to delete an issued invoice. A "void" keeps its number, is marked cancelled, and is reported as cancelled in Table 13. After the return is filed, corrections go through credit notes. Those credit notes use the **rate on the original invoice**, even across a rate change.

The September 2025 rate change shows why the rate master must be date-effective. The tax rate follows the invoice date, not the purchase date. Stock bought at the old 12% and sold after 22 Sep 2025 was billed at the new rate. The Department of Consumer Affairs let packers declare revised MRPs on unsold stock by sticker until **31 Dec 2025**, with the original MRP still visible. A later advisory made re-stickering optional, and the relaxation was challenged in the Karnataka High Court ([SS Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/government-relaxes-lmpc-compliance-after-gst-rate-revisions/); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/goods-and-service-tax/govt-mrp-revision-unsold-gst-affected-stock.html)). The schedule was amended again to align with Finance Act 2026 from 1 May 2026 ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/type/central-tax-rate-notifications/)). The exact changes were not checked, so treat the rate table as data to ship updates for, never as an enum. The domain-model research suggested a `gst_rate` enum of 0/5/12/18/28. That list predates GST 2.0 and should not be used.

Composition mode is a switch in the invoice renderer. It changes the heading to Bill of Supply, prints the mandated statement, prints no tax split, and exports turnover for CMP-08/GSTR-4. The same Bill of Supply format serves regular dealers who sell only exempt goods such as loose vegetables. The HSN master needs strict validation. From the May 2025 return period, GSTN rejects a 6-digit code made by truncating an 8-digit HSN if that 6-digit code is not itself in the tariff, so store the full 8-digit HSN and derive valid reporting codes from the official list. E-invoice and e-way bill are genuinely v2 for kiranas. They apply only to B2B invoices above the turnover threshold and to vehicle movements above ₹50,000. Counter sales that customers carry away rarely trigger an e-way bill. Several items remain unverified: the composition rate and turnover limit, the Rule 46A invoice-cum-bill-of-supply for mixed baskets, the current status of tobacco cess, and state-wise intra-state e-way limits. Confirm each with a chartered accountant before coding.

## Hardware runs on five byte-level formats

Most of the hardware work is producing exact bytes. Tiffin's ESC/POS layer covers receipts and the cash drawer already. Retail adds GS1 barcodes, scale barcodes, label languages, pole displays and Android POS vendor APIs.

**Barcodes.** Support EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A/E and Code 128. Indian-registered GTINs start with **890** ([GS1 India manual](https://gs1india.org/media/global-user-manual1.pdf)). The GS1 check digit works from the rightmost data digit, multiplying alternately by 3 and 1, and the check is (10 − sum mod 10) mod 10 ([GS1](https://www.gs1.org/services/how-calculate-check-digit-manually)). Prefixes **20–29** are restricted-circulation numbers (RCNs) for in-store use, including variable-measure items. RCNs may only be encoded in EAN-8/13 and UPC-A/E, without Application Identifiers ([GS1 GSCN 23-006](https://www.gs1.org/docs/barcodes/GSCN-23-006-RCN.pdf)). Normalise every scan to GTIN-14 as the lookup key, and expand UPC-E to UPC-A first. Classify each scan in this order: 13 digits starting 20–29 go to the scale parser; 8 digits starting 0 or 2 are internal RCN-8; 890 is an Indian GTIN; anything else is a global GTIN. Code 128 serves apparel's alphanumeric style codes and internal SKUs. Its mod-103 check is stripped by the scanner.

```
EAN-13 check digit, data d1..d12 from the left:
  sum   = (d1+d3+d5+d7+d9+d11) + 3*(d2+d4+d6+d8+d10+d12)
  check = (10 - sum % 10) % 10
  890123456789 -> 28 + 3*34 = 130 -> check 0 -> 8901234567890
```

**Scale barcodes have no Indian standard, so ship a parser, not a layout.** GS1 leaves prefix-2 layouts to national offices, and vendors disagree. SAP uses 21/22/23/27 for price and 28/29 for weight ([SAP Help](https://help.sap.com/doc/saphelp_pos23/2.3/en-US/5e/7ca28d23cc4ddf969366d803e00816/content.htm)). An Odoo module uses 21 for weight and 23 for price ([Odoo app](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/9.0/pos_barcode_scan)). Lithos POS defaults to prefix 9911 ([Lithos](https://help.lithospos.com/docs/settings/barcode-scale-label-scale)). GoFrugal's profile uses ordered fields with a letter prefix and no check digit. In its example `W00319801500`, `W` is the prefix, `003198` the item code and `01500` the weight, read as 1.500 kg at precision 3 ([GoFrugal](https://community.gofrugal.com/portal/en/kb/articles/configuration-of-a-barcode-profile-for-weighing-scale-rpos7)). Zoho POS India defines prefix, SKU length, weight length/precision and amount length/precision. In `W12123450200`, `W12` is the prefix, `12345` the SKU and `0200` the weight, 2.00 kg. Zoho warns that **weight precision must match the item's quantity decimals or prices go wrong** ([Zoho POS](https://www.zoho.com/en-in/pos/resources/help/embedded-barcode.html)). OpenSourcePOS uses tokens: `20{I:4}\d{P:5}\d` parses `2000021019409` as item 0002, a skipped price-check digit, and price 01940 ([OSPOS](https://github.com/opensourcepos/opensourcepos/wiki/Weighted-Barcodes/Configuration)). Rice Lake advises printing a test label and matching the human-readable digits, including leading zeros, against the item master ([Rice Lake](https://www.ricelake.com/media/xa0nhect/tb_determining_barcode_formats.pdf)). Manuals giving exact format tables for Essae Teraoka, CAS, Mettler Toledo or Aczet scales could not be found, so the setup screen should decode a sample label live.

```
Scale profile fields:
  prefix[] ("21","22" | "W"), symbology (EAN13|CODE128), item_start, item_len,
  value_kind (weight|price|qty), value_start, value_len, value_decimals,
  has_price_check_digit, validate_ean_check, item_lookup_mode (strip zeros?)

Default A (weight): 2 1 I I I I I W W W W W C
  21 00123 01250 3  -> "2100123012503" = PLU 123, 1.250 kg   (check: 12 + 3*5 = 27 -> 3)
Default B (price):  2 2 I I I I I P P P P P C   -> 22 00123 04550 C = Rs 45.50
Alt C:  F F I I I I I I W W W W C      (6-digit code, 4-digit weight)
Alt D:  2 F I I I I K V V V V C        (K = price-check digit to skip)
Alt E:  W IIIIII VVVVV                 (Code 128, no check; GoFrugal style)
Internal SKU EAN-13s: use a 2x prefix not claimed by any scale profile (e.g. 29).
```

On a weight barcode, qty = weight and amount = qty × rate. On a price barcode, amount = price and qty = price ÷ rate, to 3 decimals. Keep both values on the bill line. A setting decides whether the scale's price or the POS rate wins; the default is to trust the barcode.

**Label printers.** Target **TSPL first and ZPL second**. That covers TSC, TVS (sold as TSPL-compatible, though not verified from a primary source), and Chinese OEMs. Godex printers are covered through their ZPL emulation. TSPL's barcode command is `BARCODE x,y,"type",height,readable,rotation,narrow,wide,"data"`; for EAN13 the printer computes the check digit from 12 data digits ([TSC TSPL manual](https://fs.tscprinters.com/system/files/31-0000001-00_tspl_tspl2_programming_3_0.pdf)). At 203 dpi there are 8 dots/mm, so **50×25 mm = 400×200 dots and 38×25 mm = 304×200 dots**. At narrow=2 an EAN-13 is about 226 dots wide with quiet zones, which fits both sizes. The examples below are inferences that need checking on real hardware. "₹" is not in the built-in fonts, so print "Rs." or download a TTF.

```
TSPL 50x25 mm                          ZPL II 50x25 mm (^PW304 for 38 mm)
SIZE 50 mm,25 mm                       ^XA
GAP 2 mm,0 mm                          ^PW400^LL200^LH0,0^CI28
DIRECTION 1                            ^FO16,8^A0N,22,22^FDBASMATI RICE 1KG^FS
REFERENCE 0,0                          ^FO16,34^A0N,22,22^FDMRP Rs.125.00^FS
CODEPAGE 1252                          ^FO40,64^BY2^BEN,80,Y,N^FD890123456789^FS
CLS                                    ^PQ1
TEXT 16,8,"2",0,1,1,"BASMATI RICE 1KG" ^XZ
TEXT 16,34,"2",0,1,1,"MRP Rs.125.00 (Incl. of all taxes)"
BARCODE 40,64,"EAN13",80,1,0,2,2,"890123456789"
PRINT 1,1
(38x25: SIZE 38 mm,25 mm; two-up 2x38 on one liner: SIZE 80 mm and offset the second label by (38+gap)*8 dots)
```

Send these as RAW. On Windows, use the winspool sequence `OpenPrinter` → `StartDocPrinter` (datatype "RAW") → `WritePrinter` → close ([Microsoft](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/troubleshoot/developer/dotnet/framework/general/send-raw-data-to-printer)), or TCP port 9100 for networked printers. Never use the HTML print path, because GDI rasterising blurs barcodes. On Android, use USB `bulkTransfer` on a printer-class bulk OUT endpoint in chunks of 16 KB or less, or Bluetooth SPP with UUID `00001101-0000-1000-8000-00805F9B34FB`. The same transports carry ESC/POS.

**Receipt printer and cash drawer.** The drawer opens with `ESC p m t1 t2` = `1B 70 00 19 FA` on pin 2 (on 50 ms, off 500 ms) or `1B 70 01 19 FA` on pin 5. The pulse times are t × 2 ms ([Epson ESC/POS](https://download4.epson.biz/sec_pubs/pos/reference_en/escpos/esc_lp.html); these byte values are the established standard, as the page could not be fetched). Other commands to use are `1B 40` (initialise), `1D 56 42 00` (feed and partial cut), and `1D 6B 43 0D` + 13 digits (EAN-13 on the receipt). Indian printers such as TVS RP3160, Posiflex PP-8800 and Epson TM-T82 generally appear as USB printer-class devices.

**Pole display.** The Posiflex PD-2600 supports ADM, Aedex, ESC/POS, Futaba, Noritake and UTC modes, set by DIP switch. It **ships in Noritake mode** and must be switched to Epson mode for OPOS/UPOS drivers ([Posiflex](https://www2.posiflex.com/en-global/product/index/Line-Display/PD_Standalone/PD-2600-Series); [manual](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/783712/posiflex-pd-2600.html?page=10)). Implement Epson mode and CD5220 mode. Epson mode is `1B 40 0C`, then line 1 text, then `1F 24 01 02`, then line 2. CD5220 mode is `1B 40 0C`, then line 1, then `1B 6C 01 02`, then line 2 ([Epson CD5220 manual](https://www.manualsdir.com/manuals/87094/epson-cd5220.html?page=15)). Pad each line to 20 characters, use 9600 8N1 over serial or USB virtual COM, and print "Rs" because VFDs have no "₹" glyph.

**Scanners and Android POS.** On Windows, scanners are HID keyboard-wedge devices that end with Enter. Detect a scan by timing: buffer keys, reset after about 50 ms of silence, and on Enter with at least 6 characters averaging under about 30 ms per key, treat the buffer as a scan. These thresholds are common practice, not a published spec. Bepoz showed the risk: its typing-speed threshold broke manual barcode entry after an upgrade ([Oolio/Bepoz KB](https://internal.oolio.com/bepoz/i-can-no-longer-enter-a-barcode-manually-and-press-enter-within-smartpos-after-i-have-upgrade-to-4.6.1.1-and-above-keyboard-wedge-threshold_50)). Offer an optional scanner prefix character, and tell installers to set scanners to the US keyboard layout. On Sunmi devices, receive the broadcast `com.sunmi.scanner.ACTION_DATA_CODE_RECEIVED` with extra `data`, after enabling broadcast output ([Sunmi scanner guide](https://file.cdn.sunmi.com/SUNMIDOCS/Barcode-Scanner-User-Guide-20201202.pdf)). Print through the AIDL service `woyou.aidlservice.jiuiv5`, using `sendRawData(byte[])` to pass ESC/POS through and `openDrawer()` for the drawer. Declare the service in a `<queries>` block for Android 11 and later ([Sunmi print service](https://file.cdn.sunmi.com/SUNMIDOCS/Sunmi-k2-Print-Service-Development-Documentation.pdf)). Zebra DataWedge also uses configurable intents ([Zebra](https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/11-0/guide/samples/tutorial-ReceiveScannedData/)). iMin and PAX action names were not verified, so make the action string and extra name settings rather than constants.

## Batch-level MRP reshapes the data model

The QSR menu-item model breaks in retail in three places: **a SKU can sit on the shelf at several MRPs at once, a T-shirt is a template with dozens of stocked variants, and stock is a ledger rather than a counter.**

**Items and variants.** Follow Odoo's split between template and variant, where each attribute combination (Blue, S) carries its own stock and sales. Four colours × five sizes gives 20 variants. Odoo can create variants "instantly" or "dynamically", and that mode cannot change once the attribute is attached ([Odoo Variants](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/sales/sales/products_prices/products/variants.html)). Per-value "price extra" fits apparel size pricing. GoFrugal advertises **multiple EAN codes per item** ([GoFrugal supermarket](https://www.gofrugal.com/retail/erp/supermarket/)). The `item_barcode` table should therefore be one-to-many, with `uom` and `pack_qty` so that scanning a case adds 12 units. The suggested tables are `item_template`, `item` (the variant/SKU), `item_barcode`, `item_uom_conversion`, a self-referencing `category` (department > category > sub-category), `brand`, and a date-effective `hsn_rate`. Item fields should include an 8-digit HSN, UQC (NOS, PCS, KGS, LTR, MTR), cess, MRP, price, `price_includes_tax`, `is_weighed` with 3-decimal quantity, `batch_tracked`, `shelf_life_days`, `costing_method`, and per-store reorder level and quantity. ERPNext expresses pricing-rule quantities in the stock UOM and limits transaction UOMs to those with a defined conversion ([ERPNext Stock Settings](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/stock-settings)). For apparel, add season, style and design number as attributes or classification fields.

**Batches.** Use `batch(item_id, batch_no, mfg_date, expiry_date, mrp, selling_price, cost, supplier_id, grn_line_id)` with `stock_balance(store, item, batch, qty)`. Batches are created at GRN. In ERPNext, a batch's item and ID are frozen once a transaction uses them, and from v15 batch items can never go negative ([ERPNext Batch](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/batch)). The picking rule comes from Logic ERP: **auto-pick when there is a single lot, or when all lots share one MRP; otherwise open the lot picker** ([Logic ERP](https://kb.logicerp.com/logic-erp-version-release-notes/november-2019/billing/auto-pick-lot-number-if-single-lot-or-have-same-mrp)). Before picking, exclude expired batches, with a store setting to either block or require a manager override. Within a single MRP group, pick the earliest expiry first (FEFO). This is an inference, as no source documents an explicit FEFO setting. Zoho Inventory prices a new batch at cost × (1 + margin%) and offers Default/Latest/Highest/Inward pricing for existing ones ([Zoho Inventory](https://www.zoho.com/en-ng/inventory/help/advanced-inventory-tracking/batch-price-management/batch-price-management.html)). Support a revised MRP alongside the old MRP on a batch for rate-change events.

**Prices and promotions.** MRP is a hard ceiling for packaged goods under LMPC Rule 18(2), with penalties under s.36 of the LM Act ([Lok Sabha reply](https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/PMS/writereaddata/2025_LS_B_4362.pdf)). Loose goods without an MRP skip the check. Prices resolve in this order: batch or item price, then the customer-group price list, then quantity slab, then promotion, then manual discount within the cashier's role limit, then bill discount. Finally, validate the inclusive unit price against the batch MRP. "X% off MRP" needs to be its own rule type. Model promotions on ERPNext's Pricing Rule. A rule scopes to an item, group, brand or whole transaction. It applies to a customer or customer group, with min/max qty and amount and validity dates. Its result is a rate, a discount %, a discount amount, or a free product. Flags cover Mixed Conditions, Cumulative and Coupon. Priority settles conflicts, "Apply Multiple" allows stacking, and "Apply Discount on Discounted Rate" compounds percentages ([ERPNext Pricing Rule](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/pricing-rule)). Odoo adds cheapest-item discounts, next-order coupons and pricelist-restricted programs ([Odoo Loyalty/Discount](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/sales/sales/products_prices/loyalty_discount.html)). On tax, CBIC Circular 92/11/2019 treats **BOGO as one supply of two goods for one price**: GST falls on the price actually charged and no ITC reversal is needed, while genuinely free samples do require reversal ([GST Council circular](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-06/circular-cgst-92.pdf)). Show the free line at its price with a 100% discount, and allocate the bundle price across lines at each line's own rate. Check that allocation with a chartered accountant when the rates differ.

**Customers, loyalty and khata.** Key the customer on mobile number, unique per tenant, with an optional GSTIN for B2B. Loyalty follows ERPNext's model. Tiers are based on cumulative spend. A collection factor sets the amount per point (at 100, ₹1,000 earns 10 points), and a conversion factor sets the value of a redeemed point. Points have an expiry in days and post only from submitted invoices ([ERPNext Loyalty](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/loyalty-program)). Implement it as `loyalty_ledger(customer, invoice, ±points, type, expires_on)`, expire points FIFO, and reverse points pro rata on returns. Khata is a `customer_ledger` of invoice debits and receipt credits, with `credit_limit` and `credit_days`. A receipt can settle invoices oldest-first or by explicit allocation. A sale over the limit is blocked unless a manager overrides. Ageing feeds WhatsApp reminders.

**Purchases and stock.** A GRN line carries item, batch, mfg and expiry dates, MRP, qty, free qty (entering at zero cost), UOM, rate, discount, GST, landed-cost share and new selling price. A purchase return issues a debit note against the original batch. Valuation is FIFO or moving average, fixed per item once saved. Negative stock is a per-item policy and is never allowed for batch items. A "stock frozen up to" date blocks back-dated postings ([ERPNext Stock Settings](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/user/manual/en/stock-settings)). Make `stock_ledger(store, item, batch, qty_change, valuation_rate, voucher_type, voucher_id, posted_at)` append-only. Adjustments carry reason codes (damage, expiry, theft, sample, found, count error), which satisfies Rule 56's lost/stolen/destroyed categories. Transfers go dispatch → in-transit → receipt, with any shortfall posted as variance. Stock take runs as zone scan sessions → freeze snapshot → counted vs system → approval → posted variance.

**Counter operations and sync.** The research found no sources for these areas, so the following is standard POS design. Each terminal has its own FY invoice series pre-allocated for offline use. Shifts open with a denomination-counted float, record cash in and out with reasons, and close with a denomination count compared against expected amounts per tender. An X report is a mid-shift read; a Z report closes the day. Hold/recall drafts are stored per counter. A return against a bill references the original line and batch and is capped at the returnable quantity. A return without a bill needs a manager. Refunds go to the original tender or to a store-credit wallet. Model payments as `payment(invoice, mode[cash|upi|card|credit|store_credit|loyalty|wallet], amount, ref)`. For multi-store, HQ owns versioned masters (items, prices, promotions, rate table) and pushes deltas. Stores own transactions and push them up. UUID keys plus per-terminal series prevent conflicts. Invoices and credit notes are immutable, amended only by new documents, and retained for at least eight years from FY end to cover the 72-month rule.

## Speed means keyboard, scan capture and never losing data

Indian billing software is keyboard-first. GoFrugal TruePOS maps F6 to save, F7 to view records, F9 to clear, F10 to close and Ctrl+F3 to cancel a bill. Delete removes a line and Enter opens a searchable lookup list. The owner can configure tender types: cash, card, cheque, sale on credit and gift voucher ([GoFrugal TruePOS](https://community.gofrugal.com/portal/en/kb/articles/familiarizing-with-truepos-29-1-2025)). RetailEasy7 lets users **rebind function keys** ([GoFrugal](https://community.gofrugal.com/portal/en/kb/articles/how-to-modify-function-keys-in-retaileasy7)), and Hold/Recall is standard ([GoBill](https://community.gofrugal.com/portal/en/kb/articles/what-is-the-hold-recall-and-delivery-bill-in-gobill-windows)). Marg reads quantity from the barcode through a separator (`1234/10`), a fixed position or a prefix ([Marg Care](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/179436/1/How-to-search-Item-by)). It accepts several pasted barcodes at once ([Marg Care](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/2769/1/How-to-enable-multi-bar-code)) and uses `*` to mark returned lines inside a sale ([Marg Care](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp)). The new POS should therefore offer rebindable keys with GoFrugal-like defaults, a qty-in-barcode option, and an in-bill return marker, so cashiers moving from either product keep their muscle memory. Indian benchmarks for bill time or items per minute were not found, so set internal targets and measure them.

Scan handling is where speed silently fails. A wedge scanner types into whatever field has focus. Wrong focus, a mismatched keyboard layout or a hidden prefix/suffix setting can drop valid scans without any error ([JLCMC](https://jlcmc.com/blog/barcode-scanner-problem)). The scanner's own beep "doesn't communicate with application directly" and so cannot report a lookup failure ([PHP POS community](https://support.phppointofsale.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/28329397632788-Barcode-Scanner-Audible-Sound-Doesn-t-Align-with-POS)). Capture scans globally into the bill whatever has focus. Play a distinct error sound and flash the screen on an unknown code. Turn a quick repeat scan into a visible quantity increment rather than a duplicate line. Reject a scale barcode whose decimals don't match the item's unit instead of billing a wrong price. Onboarding has its own failure mode: item imports fail on missing HSN codes ([Logic ERP](https://kb.logicerp.com/logic-erp-installation-configuration-and-troubleshooting-solutions/troubleshooting/purchase/hsn-code-error-in-purchase-import)), on HSN–rate mismatches ([Busy FAQ](https://busy.in/faqs/data-conversion/voucher-import-export/25/)), and on non-unique HSN/description pairs ([Tally Help](https://help.tallysolutions.com/article/te9rel61/Tax_India/gst/gst_issues_offline_portal.htm)). The import wizard must validate HSN and rate per row, preview errors, and re-run without creating duplicates.

The complaints are about trust and latency. A five-year Vyapar user "suddenly lost data, mismatched entries in previous records" ([SoftwareAdvice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/accounting/vyapar-profile/reviews/)). Another was told to redo a year of entries ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.ca/software/2047570/vyapar)). A G2 reviewer called Vyapar "terribly slow for fast-paced retail counters" ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/vyapar/reviews_and_filters)), and Capterra's summary adds hangs, blank WhatsApp invoices and mobile–desktop sync mismatches ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/180579/Vyapar/reviews/)). GoFrugal reviewers, mostly from 2018–2021, report slowness after idle periods, "backup is very slow at the exit of the software", and slow support, though they also praise its fast billing ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/71693/GoFrugal-POS-Software/reviews/)). A myBillBook user describes an AI support bot that wrongly said they were not calling from their registered number ([featurebase](https://mybillbook.featurebase.app/p/very-disappointed-with-my-billbook)). Printer complaints were rare. The design response follows directly. Use an append-only local journal with checksums. Run automatic local and off-device backups that never block exit, and offer a self-serve restore-to-date. Keep start-up warm after idle, with background work off the billing thread. Test WhatsApp PDFs end to end. Guarantee a path to a human for support.

## Conclusion

The competitive gap is less about features than about where the line between free and paid sits, and whether offline actually works. Incumbents charge extra for exactly the capabilities a kirana uses every day: POS mode, offline sync, Tally export and loyalty. Users abandon products when offline billing or data integrity fails. A Tiffin-derived POS that ships offline parity on Windows and Android, khata, batch-MRP handling and scale and label support at the ₹3–4k/yr anchor would compete on reliability rather than on feature count. That makes the journal, backup and sync design the most important engineering investment, ahead of any single module.

Retail also moves the hard engineering from the menu into the master data. GST 2.0 showed that tax rates, MRPs and HSN validation lists change mid-year, at the government's pace rather than the vendor's. The rate master, HSN list and scale profiles should therefore be versioned data pushed from the server, not code. The remaining unknowns should be settled before build: composition limits, Rule 46A mixed invoices, tobacco cess, state e-way limits, and the exact barcode tables of the scales Indian shops use. Confirm the tax rules with a chartered accountant, and test the hardware formats on real TVS, TSC, Essae and Sunmi units.
