# Competitor modules: Indian retail POS and billing products (Nov 2026)

**Summary.**
- **Table stakes:** GST billing, barcode, stock, multi-godown or multi-location, and a mobile companion app. Even ₹3.5k/yr SME apps include these.
- **Upsells in most products:** POS counter mode, e-invoice and e-way bill, loyalty, promotions (Buy X Get Y), Tally export, extra users or registers, and offline merge across branches.
- **Differentiators:**
  - True offline billing on both Windows and Android. Vyapar markets this as its defining feature, and GoFrugal and Ginesys Zwing also claim it.
  - Weighing-scale integration.
  - ONDC and WhatsApp ordering (GoFrugal).
  - Retail-chain depth (Ginesys).
- **Price bands, India, excluding GST:**

| Segment | Products and prices |
|---|---|
| SME apps | myBillBook ₹3,490 to about ₹6,840/yr; Vyapar about ₹3–4k/yr (aggregator figures) |
| Desktop ERPs | BUSY ₹5k–50k/yr or ₹11k–22k perpetual; Marg about ₹8k–26k perpetual (dealer-quoted); Tally ₹8,100–24,300/yr or ₹22,500/₹67,500 lifetime |
| Cloud POS | Zoho POS $15–60 per location per month; Shopify ₹1,499/mo plus POS Pro ₹7,000/mo per location; Ginesys about ₹3,500 per POS user per month |

- **Not verified:** official pricing for Vyapar, Swipe, GoFrugal (the page was under maintenance), Dukaan (nothing found), and Khatabook (sources conflict).

## Which modules does each product list?

### Takeaway
Every product lists GST billing, inventory and barcodes. They differ on:
- POS counter mode
- batch and expiry
- e-invoice and e-way bill
- loyalty and promotions
- weighing scale
- ONDC and online store
- offline operation

Retail-specialist POS vendors (GoFrugal, Ginesys, Zoho POS) lead on promotions, loyalty, multi-store and peripherals. Accounting-first products (Tally, BUSY, Marg) lead on compliance and batch handling. SME mobile apps (myBillBook, Vyapar, Khatabook, OkCredit) lead on price and Android.

## Which features are universal (table stakes) vs differentiators?

### Takeaway
**Table stakes for a credible Indian retail POS in 2026:**
- GST invoicing
- inventory with barcode scan and print
- multi-device sync and a mobile owner app
- WhatsApp or PDF bill sharing
- multi-godown support

**Differentiators:**
- robust offline billing on both Android and Windows
- weighing scale and label printing
- schemes (Buy X Get Y) and loyalty
- ONDC and WhatsApp ordering
- e-invoice and e-way bill bundled cheaply
- multi-store stock transfer

### Cited Findings
- Barcode, online store, sync and mobile apps are on myBillBook's lowest paid tier. — [myBillBook pricing](https://mybillbook.in/pricing)
- Loyalty sits only on the top tier at both myBillBook and Zoho POS. GoFrugal sells it as an add-on. — [myBillBook pricing](https://mybillbook.in/pricing), [Zoho POS pricing](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html), [GoFrugal pricing](https://www.gofrugal.com/pricing.html)
- Buy X Get Y promotions are Premium-only at Zoho POS and "coming soon" at GoFrugal. — [Zoho POS pricing](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html), [GoFrugal pricing](https://www.gofrugal.com/pricing.html)
- Weighing scale is an add-on at GoFrugal and is supported on its Android and mobile POS. — [GoFrugal pricing](https://www.gofrugal.com/pricing.html), [GoFrugal retail](https://www.gofrugal.com/retail)
- GoFrugal claims native ONDC. — [GoFrugal omnichannel](https://www.gofrugal.com/omnichannel-retail-solutions.html)
- Offline billing is a headline claim for Vyapar, GoFrugal and Ginesys Zwing, and is gated off Zoho POS's free plan. — [Techjockey Vyapar](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/vyapar), [GoFrugal retail](https://www.gofrugal.com/retail), [Ginesys](https://www.ginesys.in/Retail-shop-billing-software), [Zoho POS pricing](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html)
- Customer credit ("khata") with reminders is free at OkCredit's base tier. — [OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/khatabook-alternative)

### Inferences
- **Suggested MVP:**
  - offline-first GST billing on Windows and Android
  - barcode scan and label print
  - inventory with batch and expiry
  - purchases and GRN
  - customer credit (udhaar) with WhatsApp reminders
  - WhatsApp bills
  - GSTR-1 export and a Tally export
  - weighing scale
- Offering offline mode, loyalty, schemes and Tally export without tier gating would undercut myBillBook, BUSY and Zoho POS, which charge extra for them.
- **Roadmap items:** e-invoice and e-way bill, multi-store transfer, loyalty and schemes, ONDC and online store, and an owner analytics app.
- Khata should be free in a POS so the free khata apps cannot win the counter.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of feature usage by Indian kirana stores was found. Table-stakes status is inferred from tier placement.

## What are the INR pricing tiers, and what is gated to higher tiers?

### Takeaway
There are three price bands:
- SME apps at about ₹3–7k/yr
- desktop accounting/ERP at about ₹5–27k/yr or ₹8k–67k perpetual
- cloud chain POS at about ₹1.5–3.5k+ per location or user per month

Higher tiers consistently gate POS mode, compliance (e-invoice and e-way bill), loyalty and promotions, multi-user and multi-location, and Tally export.

### Cited Findings
- myBillBook: ₹3,490 (Plus), ₹3,990 (Pro), about ₹570/mo (Pro Max). POS, e-invoice, e-way bill, loyalty and Tally export are on Pro Max only. — [myBillBook pricing](https://mybillbook.in/pricing)
- BUSY: Magic ₹5k, ₹8k, ₹12k and ₹50k per year. BUSY 21 ₹11k, ₹16.5k and ₹22k perpetual. POS, batch and e-invoice start at Standard or SMART. Offline branch merge is Enterprise only. — [BUSY pricing](https://busy.in/pricing/)
- Tally: Silver ₹8,100/yr or ₹22,500 lifetime. Gold ₹24,300/yr or ₹67,500 lifetime. — [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/buy-tally/)
- Zoho POS: $15–60 per location per month (annual). Offline, multi-location, offers and loyalty are tier-gated. — [Zoho POS pricing](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html)
- Shopify: ₹1,499 to ₹22,680/mo, plus POS Pro at ₹7,000/mo per location. — [Shopify India](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing)
- Ginesys: ₹3,500 per POS user per month. — [Ginesys pricing](https://www.ginesys.in/pricing)
- Marg: about ₹8k, ₹12.6k and ₹25k perpetual, varying by dealer. — [itforsme](https://itforsme.in/pricing/marg-erp-india)
- Vyapar: about ₹3–4k/yr for desktop, according to conflicting aggregators. — [itforsme](https://www.itforsme.in/pricing/vyapar-india), [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/vyapar)

### Inferences
- The price anchor for kirana and general stores is about ₹3–4k/yr, set by myBillBook and Vyapar. Supermarkets with several counters pay ₹10–25k+ (Marg, BUSY, GoFrugal).

### Gaps
- GoFrugal, Swipe, Dukaan and Vyapar official prices were not obtained.

## Offline operation and Android support claims

### Takeaway
Offline plus Android is claimed by Vyapar, GoFrugal, Ginesys Zwing and Zoho POS (paid plans).
- Tally, Marg and BUSY are Windows-desktop-first. BUSY's mobile app is a companion app, and Tally has no Android app on its buy page.
- myBillBook is mobile and web first, with desktop only on Pro and above. Its pricing page makes no offline claim.

### Cited Findings
- Vyapar: works offline and syncs later on Android and Windows. — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/vyapar), [Accountune](https://www.accountune.com/vyapar-vs-accountune-vs-mybillbook)
- GoFrugal: POS works without internet and syncs later. It runs on Windows, cloud, Android and iOS. — [GoFrugal retail](https://www.gofrugal.com/retail), [SoftGuide](https://www.softguide.com/program/gofrugal)
- Ginesys: offline Zwing web and mobile POS, and an offline desktop POS that syncs. — [Ginesys](https://www.ginesys.in/Retail-shop-billing-software)
- Zoho POS: offline billing and sync from the Standard plan up. Apps on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS. — [Zoho POS pricing](https://www.zoho.com/pos/pricing.html)
- BUSY: offline and online working with data merge is listed under Enterprise. Mobile App Lite comes with the Magic plans. — [BUSY pricing](https://busy.in/pricing/)
- myBillBook: Android, iOS and web on all tiers, desktop from Pro. — [myBillBook pricing](https://mybillbook.in/pricing)
- Tally: no mobile or Android app is mentioned on the buy page. Remote access is through Cloud Access. — [Tally](https://tallysolutions.com/buy-tally/)

### Inferences
- Few products offer the same full-featured offline POS on both a Windows counter and an Android device, at SME prices, without tier gating. Vyapar is the closest competitor on this claim.

### Gaps
- None of these offline claims were independently tested. How much each product can do offline (for example e-invoice queuing, or multi-device conflict handling) is undocumented in the sources read.
